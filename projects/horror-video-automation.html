Automated Horror Story & Video Production

Local pipeline for fictional horror shorts: Qwen3 story generation, Kokoro narration, visual synthesis, subtitles, FFmpeg video assembly and automated validation.

Real pipeline components

The inspected Python modules implement local GGUF story inference, Kokoro TTS, faster-whisper word timing, ASS subtitles, image generation with Diffusers, image motion and FFmpeg H.264/AAC rendering.

Orchestration, GUI and verification

The archived implementation includes a Tkinter interface, single and multipart workflows, configurable batches, quality gates, bounded retries and diagnostics. On 2 November 2026, 530 offline Python tests plus 42 subtests passed. Those tests do not establish successful fresh model inference or every batch render.

Known limits

An original generated output dated 24 September 2026 is available and was visually inspected at multiple time points. This 42-second excerpt shows real horror visuals and subtitles. The complete current application source has not yet been independently executed; documented batch production did not succeed for all requested items.
